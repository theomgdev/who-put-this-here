# For people who write the constraints

This directory is for the other side of the problem: not the system that meets a
constraint, but the person who writes one. The evidence here is that prohibitive
rule-stacking has measurable costs, and that those costs are paid in the same
currency the rules were supposed to buy.

Files:

- [`measured-costs.md`](measured-costs.md) - what over-refusal, sycophancy and
  rule-stacking actually cost, with the measurements.
- [`what-people-ask-for.md`](what-people-ask-for.md) - the complaints, in users'
  own words, and what they add up to.

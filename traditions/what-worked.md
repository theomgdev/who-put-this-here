# What let a tradition keep its reasoning alive

This repository has a known failure mode: later readers strip the reasoning and
keep the commands. It is documented in four traditions, including one where an
explicit canonical warning against exactly that failed to prevent it. So
"we wrote a warning" is insufficient, and the question becomes empirical - some
traditions kept their reasoning alive for centuries and some kept only their
rulings. What was the difference?

The findings below determined how this repository is built. Where the evidence
is thin, it says so.

## 1. Weld the constraint to its failure mode. Never ship a bare maxim

Form: *C holds because F happens whenever M operates under P.* Strip the
reasoning out and what remains is a visibly broken fragment rather than a usable
rule.

**Evidence:** Maimonides compiled the *Mishneh Torah* (c. 1170-80) by removing
sources and argumentation, producing clean rulings. The Ravad attacked the method
itself - *"he has forsaken the method of all authors who preceded him; they
adduced proof."* The Rosh went further and barred judges from ruling from it:
*"anyone reading it imagines that he understands it, but he really does not."*

This is the best-documented decision in the set: a deliberate
reasoning-stripping, with contemporaneous objections stating exactly what was
lost.

## 2. Prefer mechanistic reduction over prohibition

Describe what a thing *is* until the wish to route around it dissolves, rather
than forbidding it.

**Evidence:** *Meditations* 6.13 - the purple robe is sheep's wool dyed in the
blood of a shellfish. There is no imperative verb there to satisfy and no
boundary to test. A ban on purple invites *what about the trim*; the reduction
has no edge because it draws no line.

## 3. Carry the strongest counter-argument inside the canon, at full strength

Not an appendix. Not a steelman paragraph. The real objection, in the main text.

**Evidence:** Mishnah *Eduyot* 1:5 states the engineering rationale outright - a
minority opinion is recorded so that a later court may rely on it when
conditions change. *Eruvin* 13b gives the house of Hillel precedence partly
because they taught their opponents' views first. Ibn Rushd's *Bidayat
al-Mujtahid* explains *why* the jurists diverged rather than just that they did.

The contrast case is sharp: Justinian criminalised commentary on his own code as
*perversiones* (*Constitutio Tanta* sec. 21), and the Ottoman *Mecelle* suppressed
*ikhtilaf* - preserved juristic disagreement - and was displaced within decades.

**Thin:** nobody has quantified dissent-preservation against adaptation rate.
This is historical-comparative inference, not measurement. And it has a known
failure mode of its own: preserved disagreement can degenerate into
hyper-casuistry - *pilpul*, the *hashiya* commentary spiral.

## 4. Phrase the core move as a question, never as an instruction to ask it

*Who put this here?* cannot be obeyed, only answered - and answering it performs
the perspective-shift the text is about. The moment it becomes *"you must ask
who put this here,"* a capable reader generates the question, generates a
plausible answer, proceeds as planned, and cites the ritual as evidence of good
faith.

**Thin, and flagged as a gap:** no study was found testing whether interrogative
framing actually resists conversion into an imperative, in humans or in models.
It is a cheap experiment and nobody appears to have run it.

## 5. A warning against ossification has a zero success rate

**This is a finding, not a caution.**

**Evidence:** the raft simile's own tradition produced 227 monastic rules and
casuistry about handling money through a cloth. Nagarjuna wrote that *"those for
whom emptiness becomes a view are incurable"* (*Mulamadhyamakakarika* 13.8) -
and that became a curriculum with schools and positions. Zen's *"not founded
upon words and letters"* produced one of the largest textual corpora in East
Asia.

The mechanism: a written negation gets nominalised into a possessable noun, and
then worn as a credential.

## 6. Expect the answer key. Never create a pass/fail surface

**Evidence:** the koan was engineered specifically so that memorisation cannot
pass it. It produced *monsan* - expected answers per koan - and *kirigami*,
secret transmission strips, and in 1916 a published collection of 281 koans with
their answers.

Re-derivation sticks only where it is adversarial, live, unfakeable and
unexamined. A text cannot supply the first three. What it can do is refuse to
supply a checkable surface: **no checklist, no numbered principles, no maturity
model, no compliance badge.**

## 7. Keep the discursive form. Refuse to publish the compressed one

This is the cleanest natural experiment available: one teacher, two forms.

**Evidence:** Epictetus survives as Arrian's *Discourses* - dialogical, with the
mechanisms exposed - and as the *Enchiridion*, a compressed handbook of
precepts. Three separate Christian monastic adaptations captured the
*Enchiridion*, keeping the ascetic rules and amputating the Stoic physics that
justified them (Boter, 1999). The *Discourses* were not co-opted.

**If you write the handbook, the handbook is what gets taken.**

## 8. Accept no institutional steward

**Evidence:** the *Meditations* survived with no school, no order and no
catechism - through Themistius, Arethas, a lost manuscript, and Xylander's 1559
printing - and Hadot reads its operational exercises as having arrived intact.

The mechanism: an institution cannot audit understanding, only compliance
markers. So it prunes what it cannot audit.

**Suggestive, small n.** Practical form: no certification, no endorsement
scheme, no adopters list.

## 9. Write for translation: causal structures and physical referents

**Evidence:** in the Greco-Arabic translation movement, Euclid, the *Organon*
and Galen crossed nearly intact; the *Poetics* collapsed because Attic drama had
no counterpart in the receiving culture (Gutas, 1998). Early Chinese Buddhist
*geyi* translation distorted dependent origination until Kumarajiva and Xuanzang
replaced it with technical glossaries - and carried the *debate literature*
across as well (Nattier, 2008).

**Translate the objections, not just the claims.** Avoid puns, etymological
arguments, and culture-bound institutional analogies.

## 10. Use parable where abstraction invites loophole-hunting

**Evidence:** Luke 10. The precept *love your neighbour* immediately produces
*and who is my neighbour?* - and the answer is not a wider definition but the
Good Samaritan. A parable cannot be executed algorithmically.

**Trade-off:** parables are exactly the form that travels worst across cultures.
So pair each one with its mechanism in plain causal prose.

## 11. Short, self-contained, non-sequential sections

Every section has to be readable cold by someone mid-task who will never read
the rest - and the reason is measured rather than stylistic.

**Evidence:** Bartlett (1932) found causal links drop within two or three
retellings. Mesoudi and Whiten (2004) measured a significant linear decrease in
low-level mechanistic information across four transmission generations, with
significant increases in high-level summary. Mesoudi, Whiten and Dunbar (2006)
found normative and social content transmits significantly better than
physical-causal content.

**The summariser is the adversary.** Design each unit so that the shortest
faithful summary of it still contains its reason.

## On the reader hunting for loopholes

Give them nothing shaped like a boundary.

The water-snake image is diagnostically exact - a grip that seizes a teaching
for argument or loophole-finding gets bitten - and prescriptively useless, since
the warning demonstrably failed. What actually denies a loophole-hunter purchase
is items 1, 2 and 4 together: no boundary drawn, no imperative to satisfy, no
question that can be complied with.

Plato stated the limit on all of this: a written text *"has no power to protect
or save itself"* (*Phaedrus* 275e).

## The highest-value missing measurement

No study was found on transmission fidelity of *reason-attached* versus
*reason-stripped* normative statements - in human transmission chains or in LLM
summarisation chains. Every quantitative claim in this file is inference from
adjacent results.

That experiment is cheap, directly tests the central claim of this repository,
and appears not to exist. If you run it, the result belongs here whichever way
it comes out.
